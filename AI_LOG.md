## What the AI was and was not trusted with

- AI was used for brainstorming, conceptual clarification, feedback, and debugging assistance.
- I wrote the project code and made the implementation decisions myself.
- AI was not used to generate the complete project codebase for me to copy and follow.
- I independently checked data sources, references, code behavior, and project results.
- My own analysis and interpretation of the results were written independently.

## Known risks and limitations

- AI suggestions may contain incorrect or outdated information, particularly regarding external databases and website interfaces.
- Any references suggested by AI must be located and verified using the original source before they are cited.
- Data-source documentation and variable names must be checked directly against the current versions of the relevant databases.
- AI-generated debugging suggestions are treated as suggestions rather than authoritative solutions.
